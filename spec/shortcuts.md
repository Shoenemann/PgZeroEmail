# **Gmail Default Keyboard Shortcuts**

## **General**
- **Open keyboard shortcut help** — `?`
- **Search mail** — `/`
- **Undo last action** — `z`
- **Open conversation** — `o` or `Enter`
- **Back to threadlist** — `u`

---

## **Navigation**
- **Newer conversation** — `k`
- **Older conversation** — `j`
- **Next message in conversation** — `n`
- **Previous message in conversation** — `p`
- **Go to Inbox** — `gi`
- **Go to Starred** — `gs`
- **Go to Snoozed** — `gb`
- **Go to Sent** — `gt`
- **Go to Drafts** — `gd`
- **Go to All Mail** — `ga`
- **Go to Contacts** — `gc`
- **Go to Tasks** — `gk`
- **Go to Search Filters** — `gf`
- **Go to Label** — `gl`
- **Next page** — `gn`
- **Previous page** — `gp`

---

## **Compose & Chat**
- **Compose** — `c`
- **Compose in a new tab** — `d`
- **Reply** — `r`
- **Reply all** — `a`
- **Forward** — `f`
- **Reply in new window** — `R`
- **Reply all in new window** — `A`
- **Forward in new window** — `F`

---

## **Thread Actions**
- **Archive** — `e`
- **Delete** — `#`
- **Report spam** — `!`
- **Mute conversation** — `m`
- **Remove label** — `y`
- **Toggle star** — `s`
- **Mark as read** — `Shift + i`
- **Mark as unread** — `Shift + u`
- **Mark unread from here** — `_`
- **Mark as important** — `+` or `=`
- **Mark as not important** — `-`
- **Snooze** — `b`

---

## **Selection**
- **Select conversation** — `x`
- **Select all** — `*a`
- **Deselect all** — `*n`
- **Select read** — `*r`
- **Select unread** — `*u`
- **Select starred** — `*s`
- **Select unstarred** — `*t`

---

## **Menus**
- **Open “more actions” menu** — `.`
- **Open “move to” menu** — `v`
- **Open “label as” menu** — `l`
- **Move focus to toolbar** — `,`

---

## **Conversation View**
- **Expand all** — `;`
- **Collapse all** — `:`

---

## **Advanced Navigation (default Gmail shortcuts)**
- **Remove label & go to next conversation** — `[`
- **Remove label & go to previous conversation** — `]`
- **Archive & go to next conversation** — `{`
- **Archive & go to previous conversation** — `}`
